Glossary

Money & numbers glossary

Short, precise definitions for the terms used across our calculators and guides.

APR
Annual percentage rate: the yearly cost of borrowing, which may include certain fees.
Amortization
Paying off a loan through scheduled payments that cover interest first, then principal.
Break-even point
Sales volume where revenue equals total costs.
CAC
Customer acquisition cost: sales and marketing spend ÷ new customers.
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate: the steady yearly rate that turns a start value into an end value.
Churn
The share of customers lost in a period.
Contribution margin
Price minus variable cost per unit; what each sale contributes to fixed costs.
CPM
Cost per thousand ad impressions, paid by advertisers.
CTR
Click-through rate: clicks ÷ impressions.
DTI
Debt-to-income ratio: monthly debt payments ÷ gross monthly income.
EMI
Equated monthly instalment: a fixed loan payment covering interest and principal.
Gross margin
(Revenue − direct costs) ÷ revenue.
Inflation
The rate at which prices rise and purchasing power falls.
LTV
Customer lifetime value: gross profit expected from a customer over the relationship.
Markup
Profit ÷ cost.
Net burn
Monthly expenses minus revenue — the cash a company loses each month.
Page RPM
Ad revenue per 1,000 pageviews.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
ROI
Return on investment: (gain − cost) ÷ cost.
Rule of 72
72 ÷ annual rate ≈ years to double money.
Runway
Months until cash runs out at the current burn.
Token
A unit of text processed by an AI model; roughly ¾ of an English word on average.
Digital root
The single digit obtained by repeatedly summing a number's digits.
