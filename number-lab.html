How the Number Lab works

Math profile. We test primality and factorise numbers up to one trillion by trial division, and convert to words, binary, hexadecimal and Roman numerals.

Memorability score. A transparent heuristic: shorter numbers, fewer distinct digits, repeats, palindromes, sequential steps and repeating halves raise the score; digits widely avoided in some markets (such as 4) lower it slightly. Use it to compare options side by side, for example when choosing a business phone number or a numeric domain.

Digit symbolism. Meanings come from well-documented Chinese homophones (8 ≈ “prosper”, 6 ≈ “smooth”, 4 ≈ “death”) and popular number combinations. They are cultural traditions, not predictions.

Numerology. We show the Pythagorean digit-sum reduction (keeping master numbers 11, 22, 33) with its traditional associations, for interest and entertainment.
