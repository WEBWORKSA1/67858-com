About 67858.com

Free tools and honest explanations that help people make better decisions with numbers.

Our mission

67858.com exists to make the numbers behind everyday money decisions instant and understandable. Whether you are pricing a product, comparing loans, valuing a website or budgeting for AI tools, you should be able to see the answer — and the formula behind it — in seconds, for free.

What we publish

  • 34 calculators across money, business, websites & AI and everyday maths, each with its formula, a worked example and FAQs.
  • The Number Lab, which analyses any number's mathematics, cultural symbolism and memorability.
  • In-depth guides written in plain English with numbers you can verify.
  • A curated video library from respected educators.
  • Monthly contests that reward clear thinking with numbers.

Why “67858”?

Our name is our domain. Read aloud it is “678-58”: a rising sequence — six, seven, eight — followed by fifty-eight, which in Chinese number culture is often read as “I prosper”. We liked the idea of a name that encodes steady upward progress. It also starts with “67”, the number-meme that became a 2025 Word of the Year — a reminder that numbers can be fun.

How we stay accurate

Every calculator formula is tested against hand-worked examples before publication. See our editorial policy for methodology, corrections and independence standards.

How we are funded

The site is free to use. It is supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, partner referrals from our opt-in growth-plan form, and reader support. Advertisers never influence calculator results.
