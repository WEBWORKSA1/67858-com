Quick answer: Runway is survival time. Most investors advise raising with at least 6–9 months of runway left, because fundraising itself takes months.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Startup Runway Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Each month: Cash = Cash + Revenue − Expenses; Revenue grows by g% Simple runway = Cash ÷ (Expenses − Revenue)

Worked example

120,000 cash, 15,000 expenses and 3,000 revenue gives 10 months of simple runway; at 5% monthly revenue growth the runway extends to about 11 months.

Frequently asked questions

Gross or net burn?

Net burn (expenses minus revenue) is what drains cash; gross burn shows your cost base.

How do I extend runway?

Cut the largest fixed costs first, raise prices, or collect receivables faster.
