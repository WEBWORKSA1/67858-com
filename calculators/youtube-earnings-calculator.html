Quick answer: RPM is what the creator actually receives per thousand views after the platform's share. It varies by topic, audience country and format.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="YouTube Earnings Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Earnings = Views ÷ 1,000 × RPM

Worked example

200,000 monthly views at a 3 RPM is about 600 a month or 7,200 a year.

Frequently asked questions

RPM vs CPM?

CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions; RPM is creator revenue per 1,000 total views.

Other income?

Sponsorships, memberships and affiliate links often out-earn ads for niche channels.
