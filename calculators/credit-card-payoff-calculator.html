Quick answer: Card APRs are among the most expensive debt most people carry. Small increases in your monthly payment cut both the timeline and the interest dramatically.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Credit Card Payoff Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Each month: Balance = Balance × (1 + APR/12) − Payment Repeat until the balance reaches zero

Worked example

A 6,000 balance at 22.9% APR paid at 250 a month takes about 33 months and costs roughly 2,100 in interest.

Frequently asked questions

Avalanche or snowball?

Avalanche (highest APR first) minimises interest; snowball (smallest balance first) builds momentum. Both beat minimum payments.

Do balance transfers help?

A 0% promo can save a lot if you clear the balance before it ends — check the transfer fee.
