Quick answer: Affiliate revenue is a funnel; improving any stage multiplies the result. Earnings per visitor lets you compare affiliate pages against ad RPM.

Formula

Earnings = Visitors × CTR × Conversion × Order value × Commission

Worked example

20,000 visitors × 8% CTR × 3% conversion × 120 order × 8% commission ≈ 461 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What moves the needle most?

Buyer-intent traffic (comparisons, reviews) lifts both CTR and conversion.

Ads or affiliates?

Use both, but avoid ads near your highest-converting affiliate links.
