Formula

Price = Cost × (1 + Markup%)

Worked example

A 40 cost with 50% markup sells at 60, earning 20 profit — a 33.3% margin.

Frequently asked questions

Can markup exceed 100%?

Yes. Margin can never reach 100%, but markup can be any positive number.

Keystone pricing?

Keystone means doubling cost: 100% markup, 50% margin.
