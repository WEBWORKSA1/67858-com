Quick answer: A systematic investment plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount every month. A step-up raises that amount annually, usually in line with income — the single biggest lever after time.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Monthly Investment (SIP) Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Each month: Balance = Balance × (1 + m) + Contribution m = (1 + annual rate)^(1/12) − 1; contribution rises by the step-up % each year

Worked example

500 a month at 10% for 20 years with a 5% annual step-up invests about 198,000 and can grow to roughly 511,000 under constant returns.

Frequently asked questions

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Market returns vary; use this to compare scenarios, not as a forecast.

Why use a step-up?

Contributions that grow with income compound on a larger base each year without feeling like a bigger sacrifice.
