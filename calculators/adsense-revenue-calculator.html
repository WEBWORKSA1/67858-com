Quick answer: Page RPM bundles CTR, CPC and ad density into one number. It varies hugely by niche and audience geography — finance and insurance content typically earns far more than entertainment.

Formula

Revenue = Pageviews ÷ 1,000 × Page RPM

Worked example

50,000 monthly pageviews at an 8 RPM earns about 400 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What RPM should I expect?

Ranges are wide; check your own analytics once live. Niche, country and season matter more than the ad network.

How do I raise RPM?

Target higher-value topics, improve engagement and page speed, and serve audiences in higher-value markets.
