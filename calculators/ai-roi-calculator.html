Quick answer: AI ROI hinges on adoption more than capability. A tool that saves 4 hours a week but is used by half the team delivers half the value — model it honestly.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="AI Automation ROI Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Value = Hours saved × People × Adoption × Hourly cost × 52 ÷ 12 Net = Value − Tool cost

Worked example

4 hours × 5 people × 70% adoption × 45 per hour ≈ 2,730 of monthly value; minus 300 of tools is about 2,430 net.

Frequently asked questions

Is saved time real savings?

Only if it is redeployed to revenue work or avoids hiring; otherwise treat it as capacity.

What about quality?

Add review time for AI output to keep the estimate realistic.
