Quick answer: Simple interest is charged only on the original principal, so it grows in a straight line. It is common for short-term loans, some auto loans and fixed-coupon bonds.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Simple Interest Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

I = P × r × t Total = P + I

Worked example

5,000 at 6% for 3 years earns 5,000 × 0.06 × 3 = 900 in interest, for a total of 5,900.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from compound interest?

Compound interest earns interest on interest; simple interest never does, so it is always lower for the same rate and term over one period or more.

Can time be in months?

Yes — enter months ÷ 12, e.g. 9 months = 0.75 years.
