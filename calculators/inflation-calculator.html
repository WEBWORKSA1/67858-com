Formula

Future cost = A × (1 + r)^t Real value = A ÷ (1 + r)^t

Worked example

At 3% inflation, something costing 1,000 today costs about 1,343.92 in 10 years, and 1,000 held in cash buys only about 744.09 of today's goods.

Frequently asked questions

Which inflation rate should I use?

Use your country's recent consumer price index trend, or the central bank's target (often about 2%) for long horizons.

How do I beat inflation?

Assets whose returns exceed inflation after tax maintain purchasing power; cash usually does not over long periods.
