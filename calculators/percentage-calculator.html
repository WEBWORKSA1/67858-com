Formula

X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y A as % of B = A ÷ B × 100

Worked example

15% of 240 is 36; 36 is 15% of 240; and 240 is 15% of 1,600.

Frequently asked questions

Percent vs percentage points?

Going from 10% to 12% is a 2 percentage-point rise but a 20% relative increase.

Shortcut for 10%?

Move the decimal one place left: 10% of 240 is 24.
