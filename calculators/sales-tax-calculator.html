Quick answer: Removing tax is not the same as subtracting the rate — to extract 13% tax from a gross price you divide by 1.13.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Sales Tax / GST / VAT Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Add: Total = Net × (1 + rate) Remove: Net = Gross ÷ (1 + rate)

Worked example

100 plus 13% tax is 113. Removing 13% from 113 returns a net of 100 and tax of 13.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I just subtract 13%?

Because the tax was calculated on the smaller net amount, not on the gross.

Which rate applies to me?

Rates vary by country, province or state and by product category — check your tax authority.
