Quick answer: Break-even analysis tells you the minimum volume a business needs before it makes a cent. It is the fastest sanity check on any pricing or cost decision.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Break-Even Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Break-even units = (Fixed costs + Target profit) ÷ (Price − Variable cost) Break-even revenue = Units × Price

Worked example

Fixed costs 12,000, price 40, variable cost 16: each unit contributes 24, so you need 500 units (20,000 revenue) a month to break even.

Frequently asked questions

How do I lower break-even?

Raise price, cut variable cost or reduce fixed costs — price changes usually move it most.

Does it include tax?

No. Add tax as a fixed cost or use the target-profit field for a pre-tax goal.
