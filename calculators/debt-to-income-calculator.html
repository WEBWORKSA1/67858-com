Quick answer: Many lenders look for a total DTI around 36% or lower, and 43% is a commonly cited upper limit for qualified mortgages in the US. Exact thresholds vary by lender and country.

Formula

DTI = Monthly debt payments ÷ Gross monthly income × 100

Worked example

1,800 of monthly debts on 6,500 gross income gives a DTI of about 27.7%.

Frequently asked questions

Is rent included?

Lenders typically include housing payments; for a new mortgage they use the proposed payment.

How do I lower DTI?

Pay down revolving balances, avoid new debt before applying, or raise documented income.
