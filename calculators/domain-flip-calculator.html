Quick answer: Domain investing is a portfolio game: holding costs compound across hundreds of names, so each sale must cover the renewals of the names that never sell.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Domain Flip Profit Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Cost basis = Purchase + Renewal × Years Net sale = Sale price × (1 − Commission) Profit = Net sale − Cost basis

Worked example

Buy at 500, renew at 12 for 3 years, sell at 3,500 with 15% commission: net sale 2,975, cost 536, profit 2,439.

Frequently asked questions

What sell-through rate is typical?

Portfolio investors often report low single-digit annual sell-through; price your names to cover portfolio-wide renewals.

Should I include my time?

For a business view, yes — add an hourly cost for outbound sales effort.
