Formula

Needed = Goal − Saved × (1+i)^n Monthly = Needed × i ÷ ((1+i)^n − 1)

Worked example

To reach 50,000 in 5 years with 5,000 saved at 4%, you need roughly 662 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What if my return is zero?

The tool simply divides the remaining gap by the number of months.

Should I include an emergency fund?

Keep emergency cash separate from goal savings so a surprise expense does not derail the goal.
