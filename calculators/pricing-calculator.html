Quick answer: Set prices from the margin you need rather than from a habit markup. The equivalent markup is shown so you can communicate it to buyers or retail partners.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Price From Target Margin" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Price = Cost ÷ (1 − Target margin)

Worked example

A 35 cost at a 45% target margin requires a price of about 63.64.

Frequently asked questions

Why not cost × (1 + margin)?

That calculates markup, which yields a lower margin than you intended.

Should I round?

Psychological pricing (e.g. ending in .99 or 8 in some markets) can lift conversions — round up after calculating.
