Quick answer: Margin and markup describe the same profit from two angles. Confusing them is one of the most common pricing errors in small business — a 50% markup is only a 33.3% margin.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Profit Margin Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Profit = Price − Cost Margin % = Profit ÷ Price × 100 Markup % = Profit ÷ Cost × 100

Worked example

Cost 60, price 100: profit 40, margin 40%, markup 66.7%.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good margin?

It depends on industry: software can exceed 70% gross margin while grocery retail often runs in single digits.

Gross or net?

This is gross margin per unit. Net margin also subtracts overheads, interest and tax.
