Formula

Value per lead = Close rate × Deal value Return = (Revenue − Lead cost) ÷ Lead cost

Worked example

15% close rate on a 1,200 deal makes each lead worth 180. Buying 80 leads at 25 costs 2,000 and returns about 14,400 in revenue.

Frequently asked questions

Revenue or profit?

For a strict ceiling on cost per lead, use gross profit per deal instead of revenue.

How do I raise close rate?

Faster response times and qualifying questions in your form lift close rates more than anything else.
