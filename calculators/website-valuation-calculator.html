Quick answer: Content and ad-supported websites are commonly valued as a multiple of average monthly net profit. The multiple depends on age, traffic diversity, trend, and how much owner time the site needs.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Website Valuation Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Value = Average monthly net profit × Multiple

Worked example

A site netting 1,500 a month at a 36× multiple is valued around 54,000.

Frequently asked questions

What raises the multiple?

Longer history, growing traffic, several revenue streams, low owner hours and clean documentation.

Is the domain included?

Usually yes — premium domains can add value beyond the earnings multiple.
