Quick answer: Freelancers rarely bill 40 hours a week — admin, sales and gaps eat time. Pricing from billable hours prevents the classic under-charging trap.

Formula

Revenue = Income ÷ (1 − tax) + Expenses Rate = Revenue ÷ (Billable hours × Weeks)

Worked example

70,000 take-home at 25% tax plus 8,000 expenses requires about 101,333 revenue; at 25 billable hours for 46 weeks that is about 88 per hour.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours are billable?

Many freelancers bill 50–70% of their working hours.

Should I charge by project?

Value-based project pricing often earns more, but this rate is your floor.
