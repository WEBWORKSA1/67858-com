Quick answer: AI providers price per million tokens, usually with output tokens costing several times more than input. Long prompts and verbose outputs are the two main cost drivers.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="AI API Token Cost Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Cost/request = Input tokens × Input price/1M + Output tokens × Output price/1M Monthly = Cost/request × Requests/day × 30

Worked example

1,000 requests a day with 800 input and 400 output tokens at 3 and 15 per million costs 0.0084 per request, about 252 a month.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cut costs?

Trim system prompts, cache repeated context where your provider supports it, cap output length and route simple tasks to smaller models.

What is a token?

Roughly ¾ of an English word on average; exact counts depend on the model's tokenizer.
