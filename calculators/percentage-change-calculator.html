Percentage Change Calculator

✔ Reviewed by the 67858.com editorial teamUpdated October 27, 2026Methodology

Increase or decrease between two values, as a percent.

{% endraw %}{% include calc-out.html %}{% raw %}
Quick answer: A 25% rise followed by a 20% fall returns you to the start — percentage changes are not symmetric. The reverse change shows exactly that.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Percentage Change Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Change % = (New − Old) ÷ |Old| × 100

Worked example

From 80 to 100 is +25%; from 100 back to 80 is −20%.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the reverse different?

Each change is measured against a different starting value.

What if old is zero?

Percentage change from zero is undefined.
