Quick answer: Stacked discounts multiply rather than add: 25% then 10% is 32.5% off, not 35%.

Formula

Final = Price × (1 − d1) × (1 − d2)

Worked example

120 with 25% off then an extra 10% off costs 81 — an effective 32.5% discount.

Frequently asked questions

Does order matter?

No — multiplication is commutative, so 10% then 25% gives the same result.

Is tax applied before or after?

Usually after discounts; use the sales-tax calculator on the final price.
