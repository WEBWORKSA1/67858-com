Quick answer: Bigger packs are not always cheaper per unit. Comparing unit prices is the simplest way to stop overpaying.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Unit Price Comparison" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Unit price = Price ÷ Quantity

Worked example

4.99 for 500 g is 0.00998 per gram; 8.49 for 1,000 g is 0.00849 — the larger pack is about 14.9% cheaper per gram.

Frequently asked questions

Same units?

Make sure both quantities use the same unit before comparing.

Waste?

A cheaper unit price only helps if you use it all before it expires.
