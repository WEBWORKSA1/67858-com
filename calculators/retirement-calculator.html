Quick answer: Retirement planning is two numbers: what you accumulate and what you can safely draw. The widely discussed 4% guideline comes from historical US market studies; many planners now test 3–4% for longer retirements.
{% endraw %}{% include calc-howto.html title="Retirement Calculator" %}{% raw %}

Formula

Nest egg = Savings × (1+m)^n + monthly contributions grown at m Income = Nest egg × withdrawal rate

Worked example

A 35-year-old with 50,000 saved, adding 800 a month at 6% until 65, projects to roughly 1.07 million — about 42,700 a year at a 4% withdrawal rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is this in today's money?

No — it is nominal. Subtract expected inflation from your return to see the result in today's purchasing power.

Does it include pensions or social security?

No. Add those income sources separately to the yearly income figure.
