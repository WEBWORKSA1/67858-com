Current challenge

The Rising Run

A store sells one product at a price that climbs 6%, then 7%, then 8% over three successive months, and then is discounted 58% in a clearance sale. Is the clearance price above or below 50% of the original price, and by how many percentage points? Show your working.

Tip: our percentage change and discount calculators help you check your logic.
